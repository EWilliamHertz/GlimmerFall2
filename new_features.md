# GlimmerFall New Features & Enhancements Ideas

Here are some high-impact features we could build next to elevate the game and make it feel like a AAA digital TCG:

## 1. Automated Deck Validation & Constraints
Currently, deckbuilding is open. We can implement a rigorous validation engine that runs when you save a deck:
* Enforces exactly 40 cards.
* Max 3 copies of any specific card.
* **Faction Locking:** You pick a "Nexus/Hero" card which restricts the deck to cards of that specific faction (plus neutral cards).

## 2. Animated Booster Pack Openings
To make the "Booster" page more exciting, we can add a Hearthstone-style pack opening animation. 
* Players click a pack, it bursts open with particle effects (using Framer Motion).
* Cards appear face-down and you have to click them individually to flip them over, with mythic/rare cards glowing with different colors before being flipped.

## 3. Matchmaking & Lobbies
Instead of direct challenges, we can build a lobby system:
* "Find Match" button that pairs players of similar skill (Elo system).
* A global chat and online friends list in the bottom right corner of the screen.
* Spectator mode to watch high-level games live.

## 4. Interactive AI Tutorial
The rules are great, but a playable tutorial is better. We can create a scripted bot match that guides new players step-by-step through:
* Playing Resonance Nodes.
* Attacking the Nexus.
* Spending Energy efficiently.

## 5. Daily Quests & "Dusting" System
To keep players coming back:
* **Quests:** "Play 15 Fire Rites", "Win 3 games with Gaia's Loop". Completing them grants "Glimmer" (in-game currency).
* **Crafting:** Allow players to "dust" extra copies of cards (more than 3) to craft missing cards they need for their decks.

## 6. Profile Customization & Statistics
Give users a profile page where they can:
* Show off their favorite card as their avatar.
* View their win/loss ratio, favorite faction, and highest rank achieved.
* Display unlocked titles or cosmetic playmats for the arena.
