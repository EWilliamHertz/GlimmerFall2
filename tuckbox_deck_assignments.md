# GlimmerFall Tuck-Box Deck Assignments

This document outlines the four primary pre-constructed theme decks for GlimmerFall, detailing their featured cover art, vivid descriptions, and core decklists.

> **Note on Packaging Art:** The visual representations of the 3D cardboard tuck-boxes depicted in promotional material may differ slightly in reality due to printing constraints and final production layouts.

---

## 1. Gaia's Loop (Terra Faction)
* **Featured Cover Art:** `gaia_the_world-soul.webp`
* **Theme:** Unstoppable natural growth, giant elemental beasts, and the cyclical power of the earth.
* **Vivid Description:** Embrace the primal heartbeat of the world. *Gaia’s Loop* accelerates your Resonance capabilities at frightening speeds, allowing you to deploy colossal elemental behemoths while your opponent is still gathering their strength. Command the World-Soul and let the forest reclaim the battlefield.
* **Core Decklist Summary:**
  * 1x Gaia, the World-Soul (Mythic)
  * 3x Terraformer Root-Walker
  * 3x Spore-Laced Entangler
  * 2x Resonance Bloom (Spell)
  * *(31 other Terra cards focusing on ramp and heavy drops)*

## 2. Solar Singularity (Solari Faction)
* **Featured Cover Art:** `emberwing_courier.webp`
* **Theme:** Blinding speed, radiant fire, and overwhelming aggressive Light magic.
* **Vivid Description:** Strike with the fury of a dying star. *Solar Singularity* is a hyper-aggressive deck that utilizes fast, flying celestial entities to overwhelm the opponent's defenses before they can set up. Burn away their resources and crash through with the brilliant might of the Emberwings.
* **Core Decklist Summary:**
  * 1x Solari Sun-Caller (Mythic)
  * 3x Emberwing Courier
  * 3x Dawn Vanguard
  * 2x Blinding Radiance (Spell)
  * *(31 other Solari cards focusing on aggro and burn)*

## 3. Fractured Continuum (Aether Faction)
* **Featured Cover Art:** `reality_fracture.webp`
* **Theme:** Time manipulation, spell echoing, and disruption of reality itself.
* **Vivid Description:** Rewrite the rules of engagement. *Fractured Continuum* is a complex, spell-heavy control deck that bends time and space to its will. Copy your spells, counter your opponent's Rites, and trap their Entities in temporal loops until reality collapses entirely in your favor.
* **Core Decklist Summary:**
  * 1x Chronomancer Supreme (Mythic)
  * 3x Aetherial Echo
  * 2x Reality Fracture (Spell)
  * 3x Temporal Anomaly
  * *(31 other Aether cards focusing on control and spells)*

## 4. The Graveglass Veil (Umbri Faction)
* **Featured Cover Art:** `graveglass_oracle.webp`
* **Theme:** Necromancy, forbidden knowledge, and sacrificial shadow magic.
* **Vivid Description:** Look beyond the veil of mortality. *The Graveglass Veil* excels at utilizing the discard pile as a second hand. Sacrifice your own Entities to trigger devastating effects, gaze into the future, and overwhelm your foes with an endless tide of undying shadow horrors.
* **Core Decklist Summary:**
  * 1x Graveglass Oracle (Mythic)
  * 3x Void-Touched Assassin
  * 3x Whispering Wraith
  * 2x Veil of Shadows (Spell)
  * *(31 other Umbri cards focusing on sacrifice and graveyard recursion)*
